### 🛡️ About Me & Engineering Philosophy

> *"Data integrity and durability are never an afterthought — they are the foundation of every reliable system."*

I am a **Systems Engineering** student with solid hands-on experience in backend architectures (`.NET`, `Go`), actively steering my career path toward **Relational Database Administration (DBA) and SQL Data Infrastructure**.

Having architected concurrent and distributed systems, I possess an end-to-end understanding of how queries, transactions, and ORM abstractions impact database engines under the hood. My priority is ensuring **resilient disaster recovery, zero-data-loss workflows, and optimal query throughput**.
